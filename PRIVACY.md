# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Healthcare Clinical Workflow Analyst is a skills-only plugin. It provides instructions and reference material to ChatGPT and does not operate an external server, maintain a user database, or independently collect or transmit personal data.

Information entered into ChatGPT is handled under the privacy terms and controls of the ChatGPT account and workspace being used. Users must not provide patient identifiers, protected health information, credentials, secrets, or other sensitive data unless their organization has explicitly authorized that use and configured the appropriate controls.

The plugin is intended for general workflow and informatics analysis. It is not a clinical decision-support system and does not replace clinical, legal, privacy, security, or organizational review.

Questions or privacy concerns can be submitted through the repository's [support channel](https://github.com/Skharbi/Clinical-workflow-analysis/issues).
