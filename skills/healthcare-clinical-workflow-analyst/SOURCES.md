# Evidence and Source Registry

This file lists the authoritative foundations that inform the skill.

The skill must distinguish external guidance from local organizational policy and should verify the latest version of standards or guidance when freshness matters.

## Workflow and health IT

### Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ)

**Resource:** Workflow Assessment for Health IT Toolkit  
**Use:** Workflow definition, workflow mapping, process assessment, health IT implementation and redesign.  
**URL:** https://digital.ahrq.gov/health-it-tools-and-resources/evaluation-resources/workflow-assessment-health-it-toolkit

## Project and business analysis

### Project Management Institute (PMI)

**Resource:** The PMI Guide to Business Analysis
**Use:** Business need, stakeholder engagement, high-quality requirements, outcome focus, and
tailoring business analysis across delivery approaches.
**URL:** https://www.pmi.org/standards/business-analysis

### International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA)

**Resource:** A Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK Guide)
**Use:** Business-analysis foundation, needs and value, stakeholder context, requirements life cycle,
strategy analysis, solution evaluation, and context-appropriate techniques.
**URL:** https://www.iiba.org/career-resources/a-business-analysis-professionals-foundation-for-success/babok/

PMI and IIBA practices guide project and business-analysis discipline. They do not establish local
clinical policy, regulatory compliance, technical architecture, or patient-safety approval.

## Health IT safety

### ASTP/ONC SAFER Guides

**Resource:** SAFER Guides  
**Use:** EHR safety practices including system management, patient identification, CPOE/CDS, communication, test result follow-up, and contingency planning.  
**URL:** https://healthit.gov/clinical-quality-and-safety/safer-guides

## Medication safety

### World Health Organization

**Resource:** Medication Without Harm  
**Use:** Medication-safety framing, high-risk situations, polypharmacy, and transitions of care.  
**URL:** https://www.who.int/initiatives/medication-without-harm

## Interoperability

### HL7 International

**Resource:** FHIR Specification  
**Use:** FHIR concepts, resources, exchange patterns, security considerations, and implementation context.  
**URL:** https://hl7.org/fhir/

### HL7 International

**Resource:** HL7 Version 2 Product Suite  
**Use:** Background for event-driven healthcare messaging.  
**URL:** https://www.hl7.org/implement/standards/product_brief.cfm?product_id=185

## Evidence-use rule

When an external claim may have changed, verify the latest authoritative source before relying on it.

Do not convert general guidance into a local requirement unless the organization has adopted it or the applicable authority makes it mandatory.
